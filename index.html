Reel One

The Story

It’s the summer of 1969, and the hills are humming. Marquees burn all night on Hollywood Boulevard, transistor radios spill surf rock onto Sunset, and every diner booth holds somebody rehearsing lines for a part they haven’t booked yet.

Television is eating the picture business alive, the studio system is wheezing its last golden breath, and yet — the town has never looked better. Convertibles the color of orange sherbet. Swimming pools like turquoise postage stamps. A whole city lit like a movie set, because it is one.

This site is a love letter to that town and that summer: the neon, the billboards, the double features, and the beautiful, doomed glamour of the last golden age of Tinseltown.

— The Management
